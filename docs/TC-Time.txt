================================================================================
  FUNGSI & IMPACT MODUL, TIME
================================================================================

  Time adalah modul pencatatan jam kerja OrangeHRM, terdiri dari 3 alur:
  (1) TIMESHEET: karyawan (ESS) mengisi jam per Project + Activity per hari
      dalam satu periode mingguan, Save, lalu Submit ke atasan/admin;
  (2) TIMESHEET APPROVAL: Admin/supervisor melihat timesheet SUBMITTED di
      "Timesheets Pending Action", lalu Approve atau Reject (dgn komentar);
  (3) ATTENDANCE: Punch In/Out harian (self-service), plus Admin bisa
      melihat & melakukan proxy punch atas nama employee lain via Employee
      Records. Ditambah Reports yg membaca kembali jam yg sudah dicatat.

  IMPACT kalau ada gap di sini: data Time adalah dasar perhitungan jam
  kerja, billing project ke customer, dan (di banyak organisasi) payroll/
  lembur. Gap state machine (mis. timesheet bisa di-Approve tanpa pernah
  di-Submit karyawan, atau karyawan bisa meng-Approve timesheet-nya
  sendiri) langsung berarti jam kerja yg belum diverifikasi ikut terhitung
  dan pemisahan tugas (segregation of duties) karyawan vs approver jebol.
  Karena itu alur approval diuji CROSS-ROLE dgn akun ESS sungguhan (sesi
  browser terpisah), bukan hanya dari sisi admin.

  API ENDPOINT (CONFIRMED):
    GET  /api/v2/time/timesheets/default
         , timesheet minggu berjalan milik user yg login. Response berisi
           data.id, data.startDate (Senin), data.status.id.
    PUT  /api/v2/time/timesheets/<id>/entries
         , SAVE. Body: {entries:[{projectId, activityId, dates:{
           "yyyy-mm-dd":{duration:"hh:mm"}}}], deletedEntries:[]}.
           Response data[0].total.label = total jam (mis. "15:30").
    GET  /api/v2/time/timesheets/<id>/entries
         , dipanggil setelah load/reload My Timesheet (sesudah GET default).
    PUT  /api/v2/time/timesheets/<id>          {action:"SUBMIT"}
         , SUBMIT oleh pemilik timesheet (ESS).
    PUT  /api/v2/time/employees/<empNumber>/timesheets/<id>  {action, comment}
         , APPROVE / REJECT oleh Admin/supervisor.
    POST /api/v2/attendance/records            , Punch In
    PUT  /api/v2/attendance/records            , Punch Out (endpoint SAMA,
                                                 method beda)
    GET  /api/v2/attendance/punch-in/overlaps  , validasi async saat form
         /api/v2/attendance/punch-out/overlaps   proxy punch dibuka
    GET  /api/v2/attendance/employees/<empNumber>/records
    GET  /api/v2/time/reports/data             , data Employee Report
